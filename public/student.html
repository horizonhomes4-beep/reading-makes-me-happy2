1. Sign in

Sign in with your own Google account. Gmail access is optional and uses Google's permission screen. SMS and call records are imported only from a file you choose yourself.

2. Gmail (optional)

Read one latest email after granting Gmail read-only access. Review it before sharing.

3. Import SMS records

Choose a CSV or JSON export that you created. The browser cannot directly read your SMS inbox. Supported CSV headings include date/time, contact/name, phone/number and message/body/text. JSON may be an array of objects with the same fields.

4. Import call-history records

Choose a CSV or JSON call-history export that you created. Supported fields include date/time, contact/name, phone/number, type/direction and duration. Review and select each record before sharing.

5. Privacy

Nothing is shared until you select records and press the matching Share button. Shared records become visible to authorised instructors. Do not import or share private information belonging to someone else. You can delete your previously shared records through the student page only if the delete controls are added by your institution; contact the instructor for removal in this version.
